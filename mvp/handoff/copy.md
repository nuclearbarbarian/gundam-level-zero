## The Case (~250 words)

*Gundam Level Zero* uses the construction of a Heavyarms-class Gundam — a 20–40-ton bipedal combat platform after Trowa Barton's Heavyarms Custom — as a diagnostic benchmark on the U.S. and allied industrial and innovation base. Heavyarms is treated as a design brief, not a frame-perfect blueprint.

Heavyarms is interesting precisely because it would trigger redline effort across almost every industrial domain at once: heavy actuators, frame metallurgy at combat tonnage, bipedal locomotion at scale, sensor fusion under EW, integrated armament, multinational specialty-alloy supply, and a doctrinal case for fielding it. Asking what would have to be true for the United States and its allies to build one casts industrial-base problems in stark relief that procurement-line analysis tends to flatten.

> The project is diagnostic — what the Heavyarms exposes — and programmatic — the roadmap to reach the threshold at which it becomes possible to build one.

The argument is American-led with allied industrial integration. Baseline partners are Korea, Japan, Germany, Taiwan, the Netherlands, and Switzerland. AUKUS §1080 (NDAA FY24) and DPA Title III extend the partnership to the United Kingdom, Australia, and Canada. The People's Republic of China is firmly excluded per NSIBR (15 C.F.R. 700–709), DFARS Case 2021-D015, and the BIS §232 surface. *Gundam Level Zero* is the threshold at which the allied industrial system can produce a Heavyarms-class platform for combat use. **Not a procurement proposal.** A benchmark.

## Design language (one paragraph)

The project's design language is **MechaPDS** — the integration of the Penney Design System (1940s American trade-journal revival) with the ARPANET register (1969–78 BBN completion-report revival). PDS governs narrative, argument, and document chrome; ARPANET governs data-dense surfaces (logical maps, readiness matrices, geographic facility maps). The two registers are assigned by surface; hybrid artifacts are forbidden. The system carries a falsifiable honesty condition: a flat-graph form may only depict entities that are equivalent in the depicted dimension; every figure carries explicit acknowledgment of any gap or estimate. Both source registers are American institutional voices speaking to mixed public/technical audiences on matters of industrial and technological consequence.

## Anticipated questions + 30-sec answers

**"Is this serious?"** — DARC commissioned the paper. Defense analysts are the credibility floor.

**"Why Heavyarms specifically and not just an industrial-base study?"** — Because asking about every chokepoint at once flattens the picture. Asking what would have to be true to build one specific exotic platform makes the same chokepoints unignorable.

**"What's the design language?"** — 1940s American trade-journal revival meets 1970s BBN ARPANET completion-report aesthetic. ADR'd, not vibes.

**"How sourced is this?"** — Every claim ties back to a Source Card with full citation. The methodology is Borges → Ultan (sourcing rigor) → Inquisitor (review) → Ignatius (after-action).
